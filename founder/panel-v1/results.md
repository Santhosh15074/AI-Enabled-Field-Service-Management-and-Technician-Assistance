## What would flip a no

- A fellow service company owner or my distributor telling me they have used it with their technicians for a few months and it actually works.
- If in the free month it actually settles a customer dispute about which part was changed, and the price is a flat amount for my whole shop of around 500 rupees a month instead of per technician.
- Two or three Chennai service companies of my size that have used it for months and that I can call myself, plus a written promise that I can export all my job and AMC data if I leave.
- If after the free month at least most of my technicians are still sending reports without me chasing them, and the price comes down to a flat amount for the whole team instead of per technician.
- If in the free month my technicians actually use it on most jobs and the AMC reminders bring back even one or two renewals I would have missed, I would pay.
- If it could push the job into the brand's system too, or I could pay only for the technicians doing my own customers' AMC work, and the AMC reminders actually brought back renewals I am missing now.
- A fellow service company owner or my distributor telling me they have used it for a few months and their technicians actually press the button.
- A per-technician price close to ₹99, or a flat company rate for 25+ technicians, plus proof from the trial that the AMC reminders actually bring in renewals I would otherwise lose.
- Show me in the free month that the AMC reminders actually brought back renewals worth more than the fee, or bring the price down near ₹99 per technician.
- If another Chennai firm my size shows me their technicians still using it after three months and the AMC reminders actually bringing renewals, and you set it up after the summer season, I would try it.
- Two or three Chennai service companies my size that I can call, who have used it for six months or more, and a promise in writing that I can export all my job and AMC data if I leave.
- Another Chennai shop owner I know telling me his technicians are still using it after three months, and something in writing that my customer data stays mine and is not shared.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
