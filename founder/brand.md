# Brand: from "AITechnician" to a name owners will say out loud

Written 2026-10-20 from `marketing.md` (the positioning and the first audience), `competitors.md` (names to stay clear of) and the panel's reasons for buying.

**The brief in one line:** small Chennai AC and appliance shop owners who write up jobs at night and argue with customers about parts. The product turns what a technician **says in Tamil** into a **checked report** with a **photo of the old part**, sent on **WhatsApp**. The marketing plan says: talk about evenings, disputes and the technician's voice, never "AI".

**Why change the working name:** "AITechnician" leads with "AI", which `marketing.md` says to avoid. It also sounds like a robot replacing the technician, when the product makes the technician's own voice count, and it's long to say and spell over the phone.

## 1. Ten name candidates

| # | Name | Style | What it says | Out loud | On a brochure or a 32 px icon | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sollu** (சொல்லு, "say it / tell it") | Tamil word | the technician just says it | two syllables, every Chennai owner knows it | short, works as an icon letter "S" | the .com is for sale as a premium name, not free (below). Means little outside Tamil Nadu |
| 2 | **Pesu** (பேசு, "speak") | Tamil word | speak and it's done | easy | short | the .com and .in are taken. Close to "PESU" (PES University, Bengaluru) |
| 3 | **JobSlip** | descriptive English | the paper job slip, made digital | clear in English and Tanglish | fine | the .com and .in are taken; generic, so hard to protect |
| 4 | **Mudinjachu** (முடிஞ்சாச்சு, "it's done") | colloquial Tamil | job done, report sent | warm and local, but long | too long for an icon | hard to spell; non-Tamil staff will stumble |
| 5 | **Velai Report** (வேலை, "work") | Tamil + English | the work report | easy | ok | **confusable** with Tamil Nadu's "Velai Vaaippu" employment portal ([tnvelaivaaippu](https://housing.com/news/tnvelaivaaippu-tn-employment)): owners may think it's about hiring |
| 6 | **Kural** (குரல், "voice") | Tamil word | voice | easy | short | strongly tied to the Thirukkural, so it sounds literary, not practical |
| 7 | **FixNote** | descriptive English | a note of the fix | clear | ok | generic; many "FixNote"-style apps likely exist |
| 8 | **JobVoice** | descriptive English | voice for jobs | clear | ok | sounds like an AI-voice product; generic |
| 9 | **Santhosh Reports** | founder's name | a person stands behind it | familiar | weak as an icon | answers "who is behind this?" but ties the brand to one person, and is hard to sell or grow later |
| 10 | **Madras JobCard** | place | the Chennai job card | nostalgic | long | locks the brand to one city; "JobCard" is generic |

Names avoided on purpose: anything with **Field**, **Service**, **Shastra**, **Proxy** or **Assign**, because of Fieldproxy, Fieldy, FieldEZ, TeamShastra, Quick Assign and Service CRM in `competitors.md`.

## 2. Shortlist of three, and the checks

**Recommended: Sollu.** It *is* the product instruction ("job done? sollu"). It answers the top objection ("my technicians barely read English") in the name itself, and none of the competitors checked uses anything like it. **JobSlip** is the safe English fallback; **Pesu** is the second Tamil option.

## 3. Voice and promise

**The promise** (what an owner can count on every time):
> Every job your technician finishes becomes a report you've checked, on your customer's WhatsApp, the same day.

**Tagline, three options:**
1. **"Job done? Sollu."** (recommended: it's the instruction and the brand in three words)
2. "Say it in Tamil. Send it as a report."
3. "Your technician talks. Your customer gets the report."

**The voice in three words:**

| | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Plain** | short sentences an owner reads in a WhatsApp preview; "report", "job", "part" | "AI-powered", "platform", "solution", "seamless", "leverage" |
| **Local** | Chennai examples (rooftop units, the April rush, AMC renewals); write Tamil with a native speaker; Tanglish is fine in speech | translated-from-English Tamil; Bengaluru-startup slang |
| **Steady** | promise only what works today; give a name and a phone number; admit mistakes fast | hype, countdowns, invented numbers, "No.1" claims |

**Before and after**, one line from `pitch.md`:

- **Before:** "After each job, your technician presses one button and says what was done, in Tamil, English or a mix. The app writes the service report: the problem, the work done, the parts changed (with a photo of the old part) and the time taken."
- **After:** "Job done? Your technician presses one button and says it, in Tamil or English. Sollu writes the report, with a photo of the old part, and you tap OK before it goes to the customer."

## 4. The look (brief)

### Colour

All contrast ratios were calculated with the WCAG formula; AA needs 4.5 for normal text.

| Name | Hex | Its job | Text on it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rooftop Ink** | `#0F3D4A` | main colour: headers, the app bar, body text on light backgrounds | white 11.75 ✔, Paper 10.9 ✔ |
| **Copper** | `#B4471A` | the one action colour: the record button and calls to action. A nod to AC copper piping | white 5.44 ✔, Paper 5.05 ✔. **Never Ink on Copper (2.16 ✘)** |
| **Paper** | `#FAF6EE` | background, like a job slip | Ink 10.9 ✔, Copper 5.05 ✔ |
| *Approve Green* (functional, not brand) | `#1B6E3F` | only the "OK, send" approve button | white 6.27 ✔ |

The idea behind it: warm paper and ink, not the blue-and-gradient look of "AI software".

### Type

Both fonts are free under the SIL Open Font License, on [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com), and both cover **Tamil and Latin**, so one family works in both languages:
- **Display: Catamaran** (bold weights for headlines and the wordmark).
- **Text: Hind Madurai** (body text, reports, the app interface).

Check that both fonts render well on the cheap Android phones technicians carry, especially Tamil conjuncts at small sizes.

### Logo brief

- **It must say:** speaking → a finished report. Something like a speech bubble that is also a slip of paper, or one with a check mark in it.
- **It must work:**
  - at **32 px** as an app icon and a WhatsApp profile picture (it will mostly be seen there, as a circle);
  - one colour on a printed brochure;
  - on the corner of a report the shop's customers receive.
- **The wordmark:** "Sollu" in Catamaran Bold. Optionally the Tamil "சொல்லு" as a second line on Tamil materials.
- **Avoid:**
  - snowflakes, wrenches and gears (the AC-shop clichés every shop's own logo uses);
  - robot heads, brains, circuit lines or sparkles (the "AI" signals);
  - anything resembling the logos of TeamShastra, Fieldproxy or Zoho.

I can generate **logo concepts** with the `brandkit` skill if you want them. They would be concepts for a designer to refine, not a finished logo.

### The first five touchpoints

1. **The WhatsApp brochure / one-page site** (sollu.co.in). Paper background, Ink headline "Job done? Sollu.", one 45-second Tamil video, the price box ("one price for the whole shop"), your name and phone number. Nothing else.
2. **The "packaging" is the report the shop's customer receives.** This is the brand's most-seen surface, and it should show **the shop's name and logo first**; Sollu appears only as a small "Sent with Sollu" line. Owners won't put their name on something that advertises someone else louder than them. The report includes the job, the work done, the part changed with its photo, the time, and the technician's name.
3. **The confirmation messages:**
   - *Trial start:* "Your founding month has started. Jobs through Sollu this week: 0 of 0. You pay nothing unless 7 in 10 jobs go through it in the last two weeks."
   - *Day 30:* tells the owner plainly whether the bar was met, with the count, before any bill.
4. **The first WhatsApp Status post:** a 15-second clip, technician on a rooftop, speaking Tamil, then the report appearing on a phone. Caption: "Job done? Sollu." No music trend, no "AI".
5. **The reply to the first complaint** (most likely: "it got the Tamil wrong"):
   > "Sorry, that one's on us. Send me the report and I'll fix the word tonight. Nothing went to your customer, because you hadn't tapped OK. – Santhosh, [phone]"

   Same day, signed by a person, and naming the safeguard that worked.

## Rules followed

- No competitor's name, logo, colours or tagline is copied.
- Only fonts with a free licence (SIL OFL).
- No images or icons without a licence.
- **Not legal advice on trademarks.** File through IP India or a trademark attorney before printing anything in volume.
