# Marketing: AITechnician Founding Shop Plan

Written 2026-10-19 from `panel/results.md` (the re-test), `panel-v1/`, `competitors.md`, `offer.md`, `pricing.md` and `numbers.json`.

> **Launch date is a placeholder: Monday 1 February 2027.** "Launch" here means the first founding-shop setups. It only holds if Tamil voice reports, one-tap approval and WhatsApp delivery work by mid-January (`offer.md`, section 7). If they don't, move every date below by the same number of days. Do not market features that don't work yet.

**Constraint the whole plan respects:** you have a day job. Everything below fits **weekday evenings (about 2 hours) and weekends**. Visits are on Saturdays and Sundays.

## 1. Who to talk to first

The same 20 simulated owners heard the Founding Shop Plan; their answers decide the order (thin numbers, so treat this as direction only):

| Who | Re-test buy rate | Why they said yes |
|---|---:|---|
| **Small shops, 5–10 technicians** | **9 of 11 (82%)** | ₹999 at the time; the free month with "pay only if they use it" |
| **Owners who do the paperwork at night** | **3 of 3** | "I sit up every night writing bills and AMC dates…" |
| **Owners with customer disputes about parts** | named by most "need" buyers | the photo of the old part on WhatsApp |
| Runs everything on WhatsApp | 5 of 6 | low risk, and the report arrives *on* WhatsApp |
| Buys only on a recommendation | **0 of 3** | nothing answers them until a real shop can vouch. **Don't chase them in this campaign** |
| Brand service partners | 1 of 2 | double entry with the brand's system. **Not the first market** |

**First audience:** owners of small AC and appliance service shops in Chennai who write up jobs themselves at night and argue with customers about parts.

## 2. Positioning

**A.** For small Chennai AC service owners who spend their evenings writing up jobs and arguing with customers about which part was changed, AITechnician is the job-report app that **turns what your technician says in Tamil into a checked report with a photo of the old part, sent to the customer on WhatsApp**, unlike job apps that make technicians type in English.

**B.** For service shops that run on WhatsApp, AITechnician is the **WhatsApp report service** that gives every job a written record, unlike a WhatsApp group where nothing can be found again.

**C.** For owners whose last app was abandoned within weeks, AITechnician is the field-report tool **you pay for only once your team is actually using it**, unlike apps you pay for whether anyone opens them or not.

**Recommended: A.** It names both of the pains behind the re-test's "need" buyers, evenings and disputes (9 of 14 yeses). It also claims the one gap the competitor research found: no competitor advertises Tamil voice-to-report delivered on WhatsApp (`competitors.md`, section 5). B is how owners will *feel* it, so use it in conversation. C is the guarantee, so use it as the closer, not the headline.

**Don't sound like:**
- Fieldproxy's "AI field service software…";
- TeamShastra's "From Job to Payment, All in One Place";
- anything with "AI-powered", "platform" or "all-in-one".

Owners compare you with free WhatsApp and ₹99 typed apps (`pricing.md`, section 5). Talk about **their evenings, their disputes and their technicians' voice**, not about AI.

## 3. Channels

| Channel | Why it fits this buyer | Rough cost | How you'll know it worked |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Warm introductions plus in-person demos** (work and family contacts first, then owners they know) | Trust was the top reason to pass in both panels. A face and a phone number answer "who is behind this?" better than any ad | travel about ₹100–200 a visit; tea | ≥ 3 demos booked a week; ≥ 1 in 2 demos agrees to a trial |
| **2. Spare-parts dealers and distributors** as introducers (the shops where owners buy capacitors, gas and PCBs) | Recommendation buyers said a **distributor** telling them would flip them (P001, P010, P018). Dealers see dozens of owners a week | a disclosed thank-you of ₹1,000 a shop that **pays in month 2**, plus a free Starter account for the dealer's own service team if they have one | introductions per dealer per fortnight; at least 1 dealer actively introducing by launch +14 |
| **3. A 45-second Tamil demo video, sent person to person on WhatsApp** (and the founder's WhatsApp Status) | Owners live on WhatsApp. The video *shows* the Tamil-on-a-noisy-rooftop answer instead of promising it (P002 and P005 doubted it) | ₹0–2,000 (your phone; a technician friend on a real job, paid for their time) | replies saying "come and show me"; forwards you can see (people telling you they got it from someone else) |

**Not doing, and why:**
- **Paid Google or Meta ads.** Owners don't search for "job report app", the ₹922 a month a shop contributes can't fund cold clicks, and you can only set up about 2 shops a month.
- **JustDial, IndiaMART or Capterra listings.** Those are where owners look for *customers*, not tools. Revisit after 10 shops.
- **Instagram or YouTube creators.** Tamil AC-technician channels may reach technicians, not owners. Revisit once there are real shops to show.
- **Cold-calling lists.** Low trust, and it uses up your evenings.
- **Trade associations or expos.** Worth checking whether a Chennai refrigeration and AC traders' association exists (not verified here). Go as a visitor before paying for a stall.

## 6. Budget and numbers

**Budget for these 30 days: about ₹10,000.** It is mostly covered by the startup lines already in `numbers.json` (leaflets ₹3,000) and the monthly travel line (₹1,000).

| Item | ₹ |
|---|---:|
| Brochures and counter cards (100 + 20) | 3,000 |
| Demo video (technician's time, a cheap clip-on mic) | 2,000 |
| Travel for about 12 visits | 2,000 |
| Dealer thank-yous (3 shops × ₹1,000, paid only after the shop pays in month 2) | 3,000 |
| **Total** | **10,000** |

**The most you can pay to win a paying shop:**
- Each paying Starter shop contributes **₹922 a month** (`cfo.md`).
- The panel's "purchases in the first month" is 1.0 a buyer, which only says one sign-up. For a subscription, what matters is how many months a shop stays, and that is unknown until real shops have run. So use a **3-month payback rule**: 3 × ₹922 = **₹2,766 per paying shop**.
- If half the trials pass the guarantee (the CFO's assumption), each paying shop also carries one failed trial at ₹542. That leaves **₹2,766 − ₹542 = ₹2,224 for all marketing and travel per paying shop.**
- This campaign spends ₹10,000. To stay inside the rule it has to produce **at least 5 paying shops** (₹10,000 ÷ ₹2,224 = 4.5).

**The three numbers to watch every week:**

| Number | Healthy | Change something if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Demos booked** | ≥ 3 a week | fewer than 2 a week for two weeks running → switch effort from warm intros to dealers, or the other way round |
| **Share of jobs going through the app at trial day 14** (each shop) | ≥ 60% | below 50% → visit the shop, find the technician who's skipping it, and fix what's in the way. The guarantee bar is 70% |
| **Trials that become paying shops** | ≥ 50% (the CFO's assumption) | below 1 in 3 → the problem isn't marketing. Go back to `/founder-offer` or the product before spending more |

## Rules this plan follows

- No fake reviews, follower counts, testimonials, before-and-afters or "as seen on".
- **Panel quotes are research.** They never appear in a brochure, video or post.
- Real report samples and photos are shared only with the shop's permission, with customer details removed. That keeps the privacy promise (`offer.md`, item J) and matches India's DPDP Act. This is not legal advice.
- Dealer thank-yous are **disclosed** to the owner ("the dealer who introduced us gets ₹1,000 if you stay").
- No feature is advertised before it works on a real job.
