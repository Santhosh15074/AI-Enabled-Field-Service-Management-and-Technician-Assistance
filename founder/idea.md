# AITechnician (working name)

- What it is: AI-assisted field service management software that sorts customer complaints by urgency, suggests quick fixes, turns technicians' spoken reports into service records and flags equipment likely to fail.
- Who it is for: small service companies in Chennai with roughly 5 to 50 field technicians that repair ACs, appliances, generators or electrical systems.
- What it sells, at what price: a web app with three portals (customer, technician, admin). Price and pricing model not yet decided.
- Where and how: Chennai first, India only. Delivered as a web app. How customers will find it is not yet decided.
- Budget and constraints: to be built out after the founder's MCA, alongside a day job. India only. A first customer may be available through work or family (not confirmed). Budget not stated.
