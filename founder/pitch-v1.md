AITechnician, job reports for AC and appliance service companies in Chennai.
After each job, your technician presses one button on their phone and says what they did, in Tamil, English or a mix. The app turns it into a service report with the problem, the work done, the parts changed and the time taken.
The report goes to your customer on WhatsApp straight away, and you see every job of the day on one screen. AMC renewal dates are tracked and reminders go out on their own.
